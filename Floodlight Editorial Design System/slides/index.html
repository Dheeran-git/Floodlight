MODULE 03 / COORDINATION
Reports in on any signal. A ranked picture out.

Citizens send a voice note, a photo, or a line of text — with GPS attached. Reports queue on the phone when the network is down and sync the instant it returns. On the desk, the model triages, locates, and routes, so dispatchers act on the freshest map in the room.

Nothing is lost, and nothing waits on a connection that may not come.
